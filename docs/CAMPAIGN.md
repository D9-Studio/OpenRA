# Campaign — IRON RIFT

Two parallel campaigns telling both sides of the conflict.
Each has 6 missions progressing from the opening shock through escalation to climax.

---

## MAGEN CAMPAIGN: "Shield of Dawn"

> *You are Commander Dayan of the Southern Command. On the morning of October 7th,
> everything you thought you knew about the enemy was wrong.*

### Theme
Recover from surprise → build intelligence → systematic dismantling → escalation → decisive strike.

---

### Mission 1: "Black Sabbath" (October 7 — Defense)

**Briefing:**
The border has been breached. Paragliders, motorcycles, and tunnel squads are pouring through gaps in the fence. Your forward bases are under attack. There is no air support yet. Hold until reinforcements arrive.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Defend 3 settlement positions for 15 minutes
- PRIMARY: Evacuate civilians (escort 5 civilian trucks to extraction point)
- SECONDARY: Destroy all paraglider landing zones

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Basic infantry combat
- Building garrison (defend from houses)
- Urban warfare fundamentals

**Forces Available:**
- 2× Lohamim squads (starting)
- 1× Oketz Handler
- Reinforcements at 5 min: 2× Eitan AFV
- Reinforcements at 10 min: 4× Lohamim + 1× Namer

**Enemy Forces:**
- Waves of Mujahid
- 3× Paraglider Assault groups
- 2× Technicals
- 1× Tunnel Breach event (enemies appear from underground)

**Difficulty:** Medium (survival, time pressure)

---

### Mission 2: "Razor Fence" (Ground Offensive — Build Up)

**Briefing:**
The breach is contained. Now we push back. Establish forward operating base at the border crossing. Clear the first settlement block. Watch for IEDs and tunnel exits.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Build a forward base (Command Bunker, Barracks, War Factory)
- PRIMARY: Clear 4 enemy positions
- SECONDARY: Detect and destroy 3 tunnel exits
- SECONDARY: No more than 5 units lost (efficiency bonus)

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Base building
- Resource management
- Tunnel detection (Oketz Handler critical)
- IED awareness

**Forces Available:**
- Starting base with Barracks
- Build up freely
- 2× D9 Bulldozer provided mid-mission

**Enemy Forces:**
- Entrenched Mujahid in buildings
- IED fields
- 2× Kornet Crew in ambush positions
- Hidden tunnel exit spawning reinforcements

**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (learning base building)

---

### Mission 3: "Iron Sky" (Rocket Barrage — Defense Tech)

**Briefing:**
The enemy has activated their rocket arsenal. Hundreds of Qassams are falling on our rear areas. Deploy Iron Dome batteries and protect critical infrastructure while our forces advance.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Build and position 3 Iron Dome batteries
- PRIMARY: Protect the Airfield (must survive)
- PRIMARY: Protect the Power Plant (must survive)
- SECONDARY: Intercept 90% of incoming rockets (excellence rating)

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Iron Dome mechanics (placement, saturation, ammo)
- Air defense layering
- Prioritization (which buildings to protect)

**Forces Available:**
- Established base
- Iron Dome batteries (must build and place strategically)
- Iron Beam Platform (unlocks mid-mission)

**Enemy Forces:**
- Escalating rocket barrages (wave 1: 10, wave 2: 25, wave 3: 50)
- 2× Katyusha Truck attacks from flanks
- Ground assault in final wave (distraction while rockets fly)

**Difficulty:** Medium (resource management under pressure)

---

### Mission 4: "Eyes Everywhere" (Intelligence War)

**Briefing:**
We've stabilized the front. Now we need to find their command structure. Deploy Hermes drones, hack their communications, and identify the tunnel network. Then destroy it surgically.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Scout and reveal entire tunnel network (find all 8 exits)
- PRIMARY: Destroy the Tunnel Hub
- SECONDARY: Hack enemy Coordination Center (reveals mission 5 intel)
- SECONDARY: Lose zero air units

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Drone reconnaissance
- 8200 Hacker abilities
- Cyber warfare
- Surgical strike planning

**Forces Available:**
- Full tech tree access
- 4× Hermes 900 drones
- 2× 8200 Hackers
- 2× F-35I Adir (for strike phase)
- Ground force for final assault

**Enemy Forces:**
- Heavily tunneled map
- AA emplacements protecting tunnel hub
- Radwan Commandos guarding key positions
- Decoy structures (fake tunnel entrances)

**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard (requires patience and planning)

---

### Mission 5: "Northern Thunder" (Multi-Front — Lebanon Border)

**Briefing:**
The northern border has erupted. Hezbollah-equivalent forces are launching precision Kornet strikes and rocket salvos. Fight on two fronts: defend the north while maintaining pressure in the south.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Defend 3 northern positions against heavy assault
- PRIMARY: Launch counter-offensive: destroy 5 rocket pits
- SECONDARY: Destroy enemy Propaganda Center (reduces enemy morale bonuses)
- SECONDARY: Capture enemy supply depot (bonus resources)

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Multi-front management
- Kornet ambush danger
- David's Sling vs. cruise missiles
- Resource strain

**Forces Available:**
- Two separate base areas (north and south)
- Full tech tree
- Limited starting forces (must build up under pressure)
- Apache Saraf unlocked for first time

**Enemy Forces:**
- Northern: Heavy rocket arsenal, Kornet crews, Radwan Commandos
- Southern: Continued tunnel/IED threat
- 2× Fateh-110 ballistic missile launches (Arrow defense test)

**Difficulty:** Hard (multitasking, resource management)

---

### Mission 6: "Operation Rising Sun" (Final Strike — Iran)

**Briefing:**
The proxy war has failed to stop. Intelligence confirms a Fattah hypersonic missile is being prepared — something we cannot intercept. We must strike the launch facility before it fires. Deep behind enemy lines. Everything we have.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Destroy the Fattah launch platform before timer expires (10 minutes)
- PRIMARY: Neutralize 3 air defense sites protecting the path
- PRIMARY: At least 1 F-35I must survive to deliver the payload
- SECONDARY: Destroy the Underground Sun superweapon as well
- SECONDARY: Extract the strike team alive

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Timed pressure (superweapon countdown)
- Deep strike (limited forces, no base building)
- Combined arms coordination
- All special abilities available

**Forces Available:**
- Pre-built elite force (no base)
- 3× F-35I Adir
- 2× Apache Saraf
- 1× Sayeret squad (ground infiltration)
- 1× "Gospel" AI vehicle
- Limited reinforcement drops (2 total)

**Enemy Forces:**
- Heavy AA network
- Massive ground garrison
- The countdown timer is the real enemy
- Fattah platform itself has very heavy armor

**Difficulty:** Very Hard (racing clock, elite execution required)

---

## MUQAWAMA CAMPAIGN: "Shadow Tide"

> *You are Coordinator Al-Farsi of the Resistance Front. For decades they bombed from the sky
> with impunity. Today, the sky is not enough to save them.*

### Theme
Surprise attack → exploitation → defense against counterattack → tunnel war → escalation → desperate final gambit.

---

### Mission 1: "Flood of Return" (October 7 — Assault)

**Briefing:**
Years of preparation come down to this dawn. The fence is breached. Paragliders are in the air. Tunnel exits are open. Motorcycles are racing across the border. Overwhelm their forward positions before their air force wakes up.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Breach 3 sections of border fence (use tunnel exits + technicals)
- PRIMARY: Overrun 2 enemy forward bases before timer (8 minutes)
- SECONDARY: Capture enemy Radar Station (blinds their intel)
- SECONDARY: Reach all objectives with 50%+ of starting force surviving

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Tunnel exit deployment
- Paraglider movement (over obstacles)
- Technical rush tactics
- Speed and surprise

**Forces Available:**
- 20× Mujahid (from tunnels)
- 4× Paraglider Assault groups
- 6× Technicals with various weapons
- 3× RPG Fedayeen
- 2× Sniper Martyrs
- Pre-placed tunnel exits behind enemy lines

**Enemy Forces:**
- Small garrison (caught by surprise)
- Reinforcements arrive at 5 minutes (2× Eitan)
- Air support at 8 minutes (mission should be over by then)

**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium (overwhelming force, time is your enemy)

---

### Mission 2: "Roots of Iron" (Tunnel Establishment)

**Briefing:**
The initial assault succeeded beyond expectations. Now fortify. Build the tunnel network deeper. Establish hidden production. They will counterattack from the air — be underground when they do.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Build Tunnel Hub + 5 Tunnel Exits
- PRIMARY: Build Underground Factory
- PRIMARY: Survive 3 air strike waves without losing Tunnel Hub
- SECONDARY: Build 3 Smuggling Tunnels (economy)
- SECONDARY: Create decoy surface base (must survive 2+ strikes)

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Full tunnel network building
- Underground economy
- Decoy/deception strategy
- Surviving air superiority

**Forces Available:**
- Starting infantry force
- Tunnel Borer unit (key unit)
- Resources for base building
- Decoy structure ability

**Enemy Forces:**
- 3× Air strike waves (each larger than last)
- 1× Ground probe (sent to find tunnel network)
- Oketz Handler unit (detection threat)

**Difficulty:** Medium (building under threat)

---

### Mission 3: "A Thousand Cuts" (Harassment Campaign)

**Briefing:**
The enemy is massing for a major offensive. We cannot meet them head-on. Instead, bleed them. Rocket harassment. IED ambushes. Drone swarms. Make them pay for every meter.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Destroy 10 enemy vehicles using IEDs/ambushes
- PRIMARY: Launch 100 rockets at enemy positions (damage not required — harassment)
- PRIMARY: Destroy 2 enemy supply convoys
- SECONDARY: Hack no enemy units detected (maintain concealment throughout)
- SECONDARY: Destroy 1 Iron Dome battery

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- IED placement and timing
- Rocket harassment (quantity over accuracy)
- Ambush tactics
- Supply line interdiction

**Forces Available:**
- Established tunnel network
- Qassam Rocketeers (many)
- Tunnel Rats (IED planters)
- Shahed-136 drones (swarms)
- Kornet Crews for anti-armor ambush

**Enemy Forces:**
- Large advancing force (tanks, APCs, infantry)
- Iron Dome coverage (must saturate)
- Drone recon (trying to find your positions)
- Supply trucks following main force

**Difficulty:** Medium (hit-and-run discipline)

---

### Mission 4: "Underground Sun" (Rocket War)

**Briefing:**
The enemy has breached our outer defenses. It's time to unleash the full rocket arsenal. Deploy Katyusha trucks, Fateh-110 ballistic missiles, and saturate their Iron Dome. Overwhelm their defenses and strike their heartland.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Destroy 3 Iron Dome batteries through saturation
- PRIMARY: Hit enemy Airfield with ballistic missile (disable their air force)
- PRIMARY: Maintain rocket barrage for 5 minutes continuously
- SECONDARY: Destroy enemy Tech Center
- SECONDARY: Launch 200+ rockets total

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Katyusha Truck deployment (shoot and scoot)
- Fateh-110 ballistic missile mechanics
- Saturation math (how many rockets to overwhelm X Iron Domes)
- Shoot-and-scoot to avoid counter-battery

**Forces Available:**
- 4× Katyusha Trucks
- 2× Fateh-110 TEL
- Many Qassam Launchers (static positions)
- Shahed swarms for distraction
- Tunnel network for repositioning launchers

**Enemy Forces:**
- 3× Iron Dome batteries (must be overwhelmed)
- 1× David's Sling
- Counter-battery radar (detects launcher position after firing)
- F-35I strike sorties targeting your launchers

**Difficulty:** Medium-Hard (logistics and positioning)

---

### Mission 5: "The Net Closes" (Desperate Defense)

**Briefing:**
They've found the tunnel network. Bulldozers are advancing. Hackers are in our systems. The enemy is methodically destroying everything we built. Fight a desperate rearguard. Protect the Tunnel Hub at all costs.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Defend Tunnel Hub for 20 minutes
- PRIMARY: Destroy all 3 D9 Bulldozers before they reach tunnel exits
- SECONDARY: Rebuild destroyed tunnel exits (at least 3 must survive)
- SECONDARY: Counter-attack and destroy enemy Cyber Warfare Center

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Defensive tunnel warfare
- Priority targeting (must kill D9s)
- Rebuilding under fire
- Counter-attack opportunity windows

**Forces Available:**
- Full base (underground)
- All unit types available
- Minefields and IED traps
- Booby-trapped buildings as defensive line

**Enemy Forces:**
- 3× D9 Bulldozers (escorted, heading for tunnel exits)
- Full combined arms assault
- Oketz Handlers revealing your network
- Cyber attacks disabling your buildings periodically
- F-35I strikes on revealed positions

**Difficulty:** Hard (survival under overwhelming tech disadvantage)

---

### Mission 6: "Fattah's Wrath" (Final Gambit)

**Briefing:**
All that remains is the ultimate weapon. The Fattah hypersonic platform is ready. One missile that nothing can stop. But they are coming for it. Buy enough time for the final launch. Whatever the cost.

**Objectives:**
- PRIMARY: Protect Fattah platform until launch timer completes (12 minutes)
- PRIMARY: The Fattah must fire (protect until countdown ends)
- SECONDARY: Destroy 50% of attacking force
- SECONDARY: "The Commander" must survive

**Mechanics Introduced:**
- Superweapon protection (defend specific unit)
- All-in defensive stance
- Martyrdom tactics (sacrifice units to buy time)
- Every mechanic from previous missions combined

**Forces Available:**
- Full base + all tech
- Fattah Hypersonic Platform (must survive)
- "The Commander" hero unit
- Massive defensive force
- Drone Mothership for endless harassment

**Enemy Forces:**
- Everything Magen has: F-35Is, Apaches, Merkava columns
- Sayeret infiltration teams (cloaked, going for the platform)
- "Gospel" AI vehicle boosting their forces
- Wave after wave with increasing intensity
- This is the hardest battle in the game

**Difficulty:** Very Hard (defend against overwhelming force for 12 minutes)

```

═══════════════                        ═════════════════
Mission 1: Black Sabbath (Defend)  ←→  Mission 1: Flood of Return (Attack)
     ↓                                      ↓
Mission 2: Razor Fence (Build)         Mission 2: Roots of Iron (Build)
     ↓                                      ↓
Mission 3: Iron Sky (Air Defense)      Mission 3: A Thousand Cuts (Harass)
     ↓                                      ↓
Mission 4: Eyes Everywhere (Intel)     Mission 4: Underground Sun (Rockets)
     ↓                                      ↓

     ↓                                      ↓
```

### Narrative Mirror
- Mission 1 of each campaign is the SAME battle from opposite perspectives
- Missions 5-6 directly oppose each other (Magen attacks what Muqawama defends)
- The player experiences both sides' desperation and determination

## UNLOCKABLE BONUS MISSIONS

Scenario where Iron Dome is disabled. Magen must fight without air defense.

Scenario where all tunnels are revealed. Muqawama must fight conventionally.

2-player coop mission: Magen + Muqawama vs. a third AI faction (superpower intervention).
