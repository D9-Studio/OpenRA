## Unit Design Philosophy (Magen)

- Every unit is good at its role — there are no "fodder" units
- Units synergize: spotters + strikers, APS + armor, drones + ground
- Active protection systems mean single-hit attacks are less effective against Magen
- Air power is dominant but requires airfield infrastructure
- Population cap is lower than Muqawama — you cannot simply mass Magen units
- Losing units is genuinely painful due to cost
