# Special Mechanics — IRON RIFT

These are gameplay systems that go beyond standard RTS, making IRON RIFT feel distinct
and reflecting the unique nature of this conflict.

---

## 1. TUNNEL NETWORK (Muqawama Core Mechanic)

### Concept
Muqawama has access to an underground transit system invisible to Magen.
Tunnels allow instant movement between exits, hidden production, and surprise attacks.

### Rules
- Tunnel Hub must be built first (connects all exits)
- Tunnel Exits can be built anywhere by Tunnel Borer unit (takes 30s)
- Maximum 8 exits per player
- Units enter any exit → emerge at any other exit instantly
- All tunnel structures are invisible until detected
- Destroying the Tunnel Hub collapses the ENTIRE network

### Detection Methods (Magen)
- Oketz Handler (dog team) detects within 5-tile radius
- Radar Station "Deep Scan" upgrade reveals all tunnels for 3 seconds (cooldown 60s)
- Watchtower detects within 2-tile radius
- D9 Bulldozer can destroy tunnel entrances by driving over them

### Tactical Uses
- Flank attacks: emerge behind enemy lines
- Retreat routes: escape before counterattack arrives
- Hidden economy: Smuggling Tunnels generate income underground
- Underground production: build units immune to bombing

### Counter-Play
- Magen must invest in detection (dogs, radar upgrades)
- Once detected, tunnel exits are fragile (200 HP)
- Destroying Hub kills everything — high-risk single point of failure
- Concussive weapons (bunker busters) damage underground structures

## 2. LAYERED MISSILE DEFENSE (Magen Core Mechanic)

### Concept
Magen doesn't just dodge rockets — they shoot them down in flight.
A layered interception system handles threats at different altitudes/speeds.

### Defense Layers

| Layer | System | Targets | Range | Simultaneous |
|-------|--------|---------|-------|-------------|
| 1 (Low) | Iron Dome | Rockets, drones, mortars | 12 tiles | 3 targets |
| 2 (Medium) | David's Sling | Cruise missiles, large drones, aircraft | 18 tiles | 2 targets |
| 3 (High) | Arrow | Ballistic missiles | Entire map | 1 target |
| Special | Iron Beam | Rockets, small drones (unlimited ammo) | 8 tiles | 1 target (continuous) |

### Saturation Mechanic
- Each layer has a maximum simultaneous intercept capacity
- If incoming threats exceed capacity, excess penetrates
- Muqawama wins by launching MORE than defense can handle simultaneously
- Example: Iron Dome handles 3 rockets → launch 20 → 17 get through

### Resource Cost
- Each Tamir interceptor missile has a resource cost (restock)
- Iron Beam has zero ammo cost but overheats (time-limited)
- Muqawama rockets are nearly free → war of economics
- Strategic choice: do you intercept the $100 Qassam with a $500 Tamir?

### Counter-Play (Muqawama)
- Mass saturation (overwhelm with volume)
- Fattah hypersonic missile (bypasses ALL layers)
- EMP attacks (disable defense temporarily)
- Attack from multiple angles simultaneously

## 3. INTELLIGENCE & FOG OF WAR (Asymmetric Vision)

### Concept
Information is power. Magen sees more; Muqawama hides better.

### Magen Intelligence Advantages
- Hermes 900 drone: massive persistent sight radius
- Radar Station: reveals unit movements on minimap
- 8200 Hacker: can hack enemy buildings to spy
- Shaldag Observer: marks targets for bonus damage
- "Gospel" AI: periodic full-map reveal pulse
- All Magen units have slightly longer sight range by default

### Muqawama Concealment Advantages
- Tunnel network: completely hidden underground movement
- Civilian disguise: Mujahid units blend into civilian buildings
- Booby-trapped buildings: look like normal structures
- VBIED: disguised as civilian car until close range
- Hidden structures: underground buildings don't appear on radar
- No radar signature: most Muqawama units are "low-tech" (don't show on radar)

### The Intel Battle
- Magen wins when it can SEE everything (precision strikes on revealed targets)
- Muqawama wins when it can HIDE everything (ambushes from invisible positions)
- The mid-game is a cat-and-mouse of detection vs. concealment

## 4. ACTIVE PROTECTION SYSTEMS (APS)

### Concept
Magen's heavy vehicles don't just absorb damage — they actively destroy incoming AT missiles.

### How It Works
1. Enemy fires AT missile at Merkava/Namer
2. Trophy system detects incoming projectile
3. Explosive countermeasure fires and destroys missile in flight
4. Vehicle takes zero damage
5. 15-second cooldown before next intercept available

### Limitations
- Only stops ONE missile per cooldown cycle
- Tandem warheads (upgrade) have 50% chance to bypass
- RPG-29 / Kornet tandem variants always bypass first layer
- Does NOT stop kinetic rounds (tank shells, autocannons)
- EMP disables APS entirely for duration
- Only works on AT missiles, not bombs or artillery

### Tactical Implications
- Single Kornet shot = wasted against Trophy tank
- Solution: fire 2+ missiles simultaneously (one triggers APS, second hits)
- OR: use tandem warheads
- OR: use EMP first, then fire AT
- OR: use mass RPG-7 to waste APS charges then send big missile

## 5. URBAN WARFARE SYSTEM

### Concept
Infantry can garrison civilian buildings, creating fortified positions
that fundamentally change combat dynamics.

### Building Garrison Rules
- Most infantry can enter civilian buildings
- Garrisoned infantry gain: +50% damage reduction, increased range, cover bonus
- Building HP becomes shield (enemies must destroy building OR clear it)
- Maximum 6 infantry per building (depending on size)
- Garrisoned units fire from windows (directional, not 360°)

### Magen Garrison Behavior
- Lohamim gain accuracy bonus inside buildings
- Sayeret can stealth-garrison (enemy doesn't know building is occupied)
- Units can be ordered to hold fire (remain hidden until enemy is close)

### Muqawama Garrison Behavior
- Mujahid can hide in buildings without being detected (civilian disguise)
- Booby-trap ability: when leaving, building becomes trapped (explodes when next entered)
- RPG Fedayeen: devastating anti-armor ambushes from buildings
- Radwan: smoke + garrison = nearly unkillable defensive position

### Clearing Garrisoned Buildings
- Incendiary weapons force evacuation
- Heavy explosives destroy building entirely (kills garrison)
- D9 Bulldozer can demolish buildings with garrison inside
- Flashbang entry (Sayeret ability): stun garrison → close assault
- Thermobaric weapons: clear building without full demolition

## 6. CYBER WARFARE

### Concept
Magen has a powerful cyber warfare capability that disrupts enemy operations
at a strategic level. Muqawama is mostly defenseless against it.

### Cyber Abilities (Magen Only)

| Ability | Source | Effect | Cooldown |
|---------|--------|--------|----------|
| **System Crash** | Cyber Warfare Center | Disables 1 enemy building for 30s | 90s |
| **Drone Hijack** | 8200 Hacker (unit) | Takes control of 1 enemy drone permanently | 120s |
| **Radar Jam** | Cyber Warfare Center (passive) | Enemy minimap is partially scrambled in radius | Always on |
| **Ghost Protocol** | 8200 Hacker (unit) | Makes target friendly unit invisible to enemy radar for 30s | 60s |
| **Blackout** | Tech Center (superability) | Disables ALL enemy power for 15s | 300s |

### Muqawama Counter
- Build generators underground (immune to cyber targeting)
- Redundant power (many small generators vs. few large ones)
- Decoy buildings (waste enemy cyber attacks on fakes)
- Human intelligence: Muqawama doesn't rely on electronics (low-tech doctrine)
- Kill the 8200 Hacker units (fragile, short-range)

## 7. ESCALATION SYSTEM (Game Phases)

### Concept
The match progresses through escalation phases that unlock increasingly
devastating options for both sides.

### Phase Progression

| Phase | Time | Name | What Unlocks |
|-------|------|------|-------------|
| Phase 1 | 0–5 min | **Mobilization** | Basic units, light defenses, economy |
| Phase 2 | 5–12 min | **Escalation** | T2 units, main battle tanks, Iron Dome, tunnels expand |
| Phase 3 | 12–20 min | **Total War** | T3 units, superweapons charging, strategic strikes |
| Phase 4 | 20+ min | **Armageddon** | Superweapons fire, no holds barred, match decides |

### Escalation Triggers (Alternative)
Instead of pure time-based, escalation can be triggered by:
- First player to reach certain kill count advances phase for everyone
- Destroying enemy key structure triggers next phase
- Optional: both players must "vote" to escalate (prevents rushing)

### Phase Effects
- Phase 2: Resource multiplier increases (faster economy)
- Phase 3: Veteran units gain XP faster
- Phase 4: All cooldowns reduced by 30%; production speed +50%

## 8. VETERANCY SYSTEM

### Concept
Units that survive combat become more dangerous over time.

### XP Gain
- Kill enemy unit = XP equal to target's cost
- Survive X seconds in combat = passive XP
- Complete special action (Sayeret sabotage, Hacker hack, etc.) = bonus XP

### Veterancy Levels

| Level | Requirement | Bonus |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Rookie | Start | Base stats |
| Regular | 500 XP | +10% HP, +10% accuracy |
| Veteran | 1500 XP | +20% HP, +15% accuracy, +10% speed |
| Elite | 4000 XP | +30% HP, +20% accuracy, +15% speed, self-heal |
| Hero | 10000 XP | +50% all stats, unique ability unlocked, named unit |

### Hero Units (Veterancy Reward)
When a unit reaches Hero rank, it gains a unique name and special ability:
- Hero Merkava: "Fortress Mode" — deploys into immobile turret with 360° engagement
- Hero Radwan: "Ghost" — permanently cloaked, even while attacking
- Hero Shahed Swarm: "Hive Mind" — if one detects target, all lock on

## 9. SUPPLY & LOGISTICS (Optional Realism Mode)

### Concept
Units require ammunition and fuel. Without resupply, they become ineffective.

### Rules (When Enabled)
- All combat units have ammo counter
- Vehicles have fuel counter (run out = immobile)
- Resupply via: return to base / supply truck / airdrop
- Magen supply trucks are armored but slow
- Muqawama uses tunnel network for instant resupply to connected exits

### Strategic Impact
- Cannot simply park units indefinitely on enemy border
- Long-range raids must plan for ammunition
- Supply lines become attackable targets
- Encourages dynamic front-line movement

## 10. DAY/NIGHT CYCLE (Optional)

### Concept
24-hour cycle affects visibility and certain unit bonuses.

### Day Phase (60% of cycle)
- Full sight ranges for all units
- Solar Array generates extra power (Magen)
- Drone visibility is higher (easier to shoot down)

### Night Phase (40% of cycle)
- All sight ranges reduced by 40%
- Cloaked/hidden units are even harder to detect
- Muqawama units gain +15% speed (used to operating at night)
- Magen thermal optics upgrade: negates night penalty for equipped units
- Paraglider assaults more effective (harder to see coming)

## Mechanic Interaction Matrix

How the special systems interact with each other:

| System | Enhanced By | Countered By |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| Tunnels | Night (harder to detect), Urban (hide exits in buildings) | Detection dogs, Deep Scan, D9 demolition |
| Missile Defense | Multiple layers stacking, Cyber (disable enemy AA) | Saturation, Fattah hypersonic, EMP |
| APS | Multiple Trophy vehicles supporting each other | Tandem warheads, mass fire, EMP |
| Urban Warfare | Tunnels (reinforce from below), Night (harder to clear) | Incendiary, D9, thermobaric |
| Cyber | Intelligence advantage (see more = hack more) | Low-tech doctrine, redundancy, killing hackers |
| Veterancy | Longer games favor elite survivors | Suicide units don't care about XP |
