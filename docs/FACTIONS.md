# Factions — IRON RIFT

---

## Faction 1: MAGEN (The Shield)

> *"One soldier. One bullet. One target."*

### Identity

Magen is a technologically advanced military power forged by decades of existential conflict. Surrounded on all sides, they have perfected the doctrine of **precision dominance**: every unit is expensive but devastatingly effective. Their military philosophy is built on three pillars — intelligence supremacy, layered defense, and decisive surgical strikes.

They fight outnumbered but never outmatched.

### Real-World Inspiration

Based on the IDF's operational doctrine during 2023–2026:
- Iron Dome / David's Sling / Arrow layered missile defense
- Merkava Mk4 with Trophy active protection
- Hermes/Heron drone fleet
- Unit 8200 signals intelligence
- Iron Beam directed-energy weapon
- AI-assisted targeting ("Gospel" system)
- Autonomous unmanned ground vehicles (converted M113, D9)
- F-35I stealth strike aircraft
- Namer/Eitan heavy APCs
- Spike missile family
- Special forces (Sayeret Matkal, Shayetet 13, Shaldag)

### Faction Doctrine

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Economy** | Expensive units, efficient resource extraction, quality over quantity |
| **Defense** | Layered missile defense intercepts enemy rockets; active protection on armor |
| **Offense** | Precision strikes, surgical air raids, special ops insertions |
| **Intel** | Superior vision range, reveals enemy tunnels, cyber disruption abilities |
| **Weakness** | Cannot sustain prolonged attrition; each unit lost is painful |

### Playstyle

- Build layered defenses early
- Scout aggressively with drones and recon units
- Strike surgically at enemy production and command
- Win through decisive technological superiority
- Avoid prolonged grinding engagements

### Tech Tiers

| Tier | Name | Unlocks |
|------|------|---------|
| T1 | Mobilization | Basic infantry, light vehicles, Iron Dome battery |
| T2 | Escalation | Main battle tanks, attack drones, David's Sling |
| T3 | Dominance | Stealth jets, autonomous swarms, Iron Beam, cyber weapons |

### Signature Abilities

- **Iron Curtain** — activate layered missile defense dome over an area (blocks all projectiles for 8 seconds)
- **Gospel Strike** — AI marks all enemy units in fog of war for 15 seconds
- **Phantom Protocol** — deploy cloaked special forces behind enemy lines

## Faction 2: MUQAWAMA (The Resistance)

> *"A thousand cuts. A thousand tunnels. We are everywhere."*

### Identity

Muqawama is a decentralized network of militant forces united by shared ideology and Iranian-backed coordination. They cannot match Magen's technology unit-for-unit, so they fight asymmetrically — using tunnels, cheap drones, mass rockets, deception, and sacrificial swarm tactics. Their strength is in numbers, surprise, and willingness to fight from the shadows.

They fight outgunned but never outmaneuvered.

### Real-World Inspiration

Based on the combined capabilities of Iran's proxy network (2023–2026):
- Hamas tunnel networks (hundreds of km beneath Gaza)
- Qassam/M-75/Ayyash rocket arsenal
- Hezbollah's Kornet ATGM and 150,000+ rocket stockpile
- Iran's Shahed-136 one-way attack drones
- Iranian ballistic missiles (Emad, Fattah, Sejjil)
- Houthi anti-ship missiles and long-range drones
- RPG/IED/booby trap urban warfare
- Paraglider/motorcycle rapid assault tactics
- Underground missile cities
- IRGC Quds Force coordination
- Radwan special forces
- Drone-dropped munitions

### Faction Doctrine

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Economy** | Cheap units, smuggling income, underground production immune to bombing |
| **Defense** | Tunnel networks provide hidden mobility; booby traps slow enemy advances |
| **Offense** | Rocket saturation, swarm drones, surprise tunnel emergence attacks |
| **Intel** | Human intelligence network; hidden structures; deception and ambush |
| **Weakness** | Individual units are fragile; vulnerable once tunnels are exposed; slow to rebuild command |

### Playstyle

- Establish tunnel network early for hidden mobility
- Harass with cheap rockets and drones constantly
- Mass expendable units for overwhelming attacks
- Use deception (decoys, hidden bases) to survive airstrikes
- Win through attrition and exhausting enemy resources

### Tech Tiers

| Tier | Name | Unlocks |
|------|------|---------|
| T1 | Mobilization | Militia infantry, technicals, basic rockets, tunnel entrance |
| T2 | Escalation | Anti-tank teams, swarm drones, fortified tunnels, Kornet launchers |
| T3 | Cataclysm | Ballistic missiles, drone motherships, underground factories, martyrdom units |

### Signature Abilities

- **Tunnel Breach** — spawn infantry squad from any revealed tunnel exit behind enemy lines
- **Rocket Rain** — launch 50 unguided rockets at target area (overwhelms point-defense)
- **Martyrdom Protocol** — sacrifice a unit to trigger massive area explosion
