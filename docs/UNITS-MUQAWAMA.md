All Muqawama units are cheap, fast to produce, and designed for asymmetric warfare.
Individually weak, collectively overwhelming.

## Unit Design Philosophy (Muqawama)

- Individual units are weak and disposable — strength comes from mass
- Tunnel network provides hidden mobility that Magen cannot match underground
- Economy is cheaper but less efficient — compensated by hidden smuggling structures
- Drone swarms are designed to overwhelm, not to individually compete
- Kamikaze/suicide units provide burst damage that forces Magen to spread defenses thin
- Deception (disguised units, hidden buildings, tunnel exits) is the core advantage
- Population cap is much higher — you CAN mass units cheaply
- Losing individual units is expected and acceptable
